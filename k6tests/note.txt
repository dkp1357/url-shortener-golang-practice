Method 1: Clean CSV Log (Recommended for Excel / Graphs / Reports)

Run this one-liner on your Azure VM inside a terminal (or inside tmux/screen) before starting your k6 test:

bash
# Print CSV headers
echo "Timestamp,Container,CPU_Perc,Mem_Usage,Mem_Perc,Net_IO,Block_IO" > docker_stats.csv
# Continuously log stats with a real timestamp every 1 second
while true; do
  ts=$(date -u +"%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ")
  docker stats --no-stream --format "$ts,{{.Name}},{{.CPUPerc}},{{.MemUsage}},{{.MemPerc}},{{.NetIO}},{{.BlockIO}}" >> docker_stats.csv
  sleep 1
done
Why this is great:
You get clean rows with an ISO 8601 timestamp (2026-11-16T18:40:00Z).
Easily imported into Excel, Google Sheets, or Python/Pandas to plot CPU/Memory vs. k6 stages.
When done, just press Ctrl + C.
